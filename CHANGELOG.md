# Changelog

Every King for Android release, newest first.

## 1.10.0: first Android release (16 October 2026)

King's core creative tools, on your phone, built from King 1.10.0 for desktop.

- **Image:** generate product shots, lifestyle photos and ad images with your Products, Characters and Environments as references. Pick the AI model, size, aspect ratio and how many images. King tells you the estimated cost and asks before anything is paid.
- **Video:** turn an image (or a Product's main photo) into a short video clip.
- **Library:** all your images and videos in one place, with a Starred tab for the best ones.
- **Brand:** your Products, Characters and Environments, saved with their photos from your phone's photo picker. Includes reference sheets, so every shot stays consistent.
- **Templates:** all 189 Marketing Templates. Tap one to use it on Image.
- **APIs:** add your fal.ai and kie.ai keys. They're encrypted with your phone's secure key store and never leave the phone, except when sent to that service.
- Made for phones: bottom tabs, full-screen windows, big tap targets. Android's Back button closes windows and returns to Image.
- No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything is stored on your phone, and Android doesn't back it up to the cloud.
- Needs Android 7.0 or newer.

Not on the phone yet (use the desktop app for these): campaigns, ad accounts, stores, the video editor, music, moodboards, 3D models and the Ask King assistant.
