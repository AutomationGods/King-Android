# King for Android

King is the desktop command center for e-commerce creative. King for Android puts its core creative tools on your phone: generate product shots and video clips with your saved Products, Characters and Environments, keep everything in your Library, and start from hundreds of Marketing Templates.

This page is for **Android phones**. The Windows version is at [King for Windows](https://github.com/AutomationGods/King-Windows); the Mac version updates itself.

## Download

**[⬇ Download King-1.10.0.apk](https://github.com/AutomationGods/King-Android/releases/download/v1.10.0/King-1.10.0.apk)** (27 MB)

All versions and their notes: [Releases](https://github.com/AutomationGods/King-Android/releases/latest).

You need Android 7.0 or newer.

## Install

1. Tap the download link above on your phone.
2. If Chrome says the file might be harmful, tap **Download anyway**.
3. Open the downloaded file. If Android says your phone isn't allowed to install unknown apps from this source, tap **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this source**, then go back.
4. Tap **Install**. If Google Play Protect says it doesn't recognise the app, tap **More details**, then **Install anyway**.
5. Open **King** from your home screen.

## Get started

1. Open the menu (☰, top right), go to **APIs**, and paste your [fal.ai key](https://fal.ai/dashboard/keys) (or a [kie.ai key](https://kie.ai/api-key)). Keys stay on your phone.
2. Go to **Brand** and add a Product with a photo or two.
3. Go to **Image**, pick your Product, describe the shot, and tap **Generate**. King tells you before anything costs money.

## What's on the phone

| Tab | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Image** | Generate product shots, lifestyle photos and ad images with your Products, Characters and Environments. |
| **Video** | Turn an image into a short video clip. |
| **Library** | All your images and videos, plus the ones you starred. |
| **Brand** | Your Products, Characters and Environments, saved once with their photos so every shot stays consistent. |
| **Templates** | Hundreds of ready-made Marketing Templates. Tap one to use it. |

Campaigns, ad accounts, stores, the video editor and the other pro tools are in the desktop app.

## Update

Download the new APK from this page and install it over the old one. Your library, settings and keys are kept.

## Uninstall

Long-press **King** → **Uninstall**. This deletes everything King stored on your phone.
