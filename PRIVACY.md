# Privacy policy: King for Android

_Last updated: 16 October 2026_

King for Android ("King", "the app") is published by AutomationGods. This page explains what the app does with your information.

## The short version

- King has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- AutomationGods runs no servers for King and never receives your data.
- Everything you make and save stays on your phone.
- When you generate an image or video, King sends your request straight from your phone to the AI service you chose, using your own API key.

## What stays on your phone

King saves these in the app's private storage on your phone, where other apps can't read them:

- the images and videos you generate, and the photos you add
- your Products, Characters and Environments, and their reference photos
- your prompts, starred items and settings
- the API keys you enter on the APIs page

None of this is sent to AutomationGods.

## What is sent to AI services

King works with AI services that you set up yourself, such as fal.ai, kie.ai and OpenRouter. When you start a generation, King sends that service what it needs to do the job, using your API key:

- your prompt
- any reference images you attached (for example, product or character photos)
- the settings you picked (for example, size and AI model)

This only happens when you ask for it. The data is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Each service handles it under its own terms and privacy policy, and any charges are billed to your account with them. King tells you before it starts anything that costs money.

- fal.ai: https://fal.ai/legal/privacy-policy
- kie.ai: https://kie.ai/privacy-policy
- OpenRouter: https://openrouter.ai/privacy

## Permissions

King asks for no special permissions. It only uses the internet, to reach the AI services above. When you add photos, Android's own photo picker shares only the photos you choose. King doesn't use your location, contacts, microphone or camera. It's set to stay out of Android's cloud backup, so what it stores never leaves your phone that way.

## Deleting your data

You can delete any image, video, Product, Character or Environment in the app. To remove an API key, clear it on the APIs page. Uninstalling King deletes everything it stored on your phone.

## Children

King isn't meant for children under 13 and doesn't knowingly handle their information.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
